FAQ

Do I need a visa?

It depends on your passport. Check the official immigration website before you book.

What currency is used?

Philippine pesos (PHP). Bring cash for small towns.

When is the best time to go?

Roughly December to May is drier. June to November is the rainy and typhoon season.

Is it safe to travel?

Most trips are trouble free. Watch your bag in crowds and check your government's travel advice.
